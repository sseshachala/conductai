What Guard enforces

Policies your security team will recognise.

Guard's policy categories map directly to the controls your InfoSec and compliance teams already care about.

💥

Destructive Operations

BLOCK by default

Any AI-suggested command that could irrecoverably delete data, infrastructure, or configuration is blocked before it reaches the tool call.

rm -rfDROP TABLEkubectl delete
🔑

Secrets & Credentials

BLOCK by default

Guard detects when secrets are about to be sent to an AI model and blocks the call. Credentials never leave your machine via AI context.

AWS keys.env filesprivate tokens
🚀

Production Gates

APPROVAL required

Production deployments and irreversible infrastructure changes require explicit human approval — even when triggered by an AI workflow.

deploy --prodterraform destroypush --force
💸

Spend Controls

AUDIT + hard cap

Hard caps per developer per month. When a developer hits their limit, the next AI call is blocked at the source — not just flagged after the fact.

per-dev hard capsalert thresholdsdaily limits
