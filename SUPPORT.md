# Support

The right place to reach us depends on what you're doing.

## Questions and discussions

Open a thread in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/sseshachala/conductai/discussions).
Usage questions, design ideas, "does Conduct do X" — that's the right
home.

## Bugs and feature requests

Open an [issue](https://github.com/sseshachala/conductai/issues/new/choose).
Templates are set up so you land on the right form.

## Security vulnerabilities

Do **not** open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) —
email **hello@conductai.ai** and we'll respond within two business
days.

## Commercial support

For evaluations, enterprise deployments, or licensing outside the
[FSL-1.1-MIT](./LICENSE) permitted-use grant, email
**hello@conductai.ai** or book a call at
[cal.com/sudhi-seshachala-pks7pd](https://cal.com/sudhi-seshachala-pks7pd).

  the repo root.
